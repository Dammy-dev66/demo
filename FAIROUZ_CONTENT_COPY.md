## About Page

Heading: Fairouz Elbahr

Bio: I grew up in Cairo, in a home where art was a constant presence. Observing my father's paintings and our early conversations about visual form established the foundation of how I perceive colour and space.

Bio continuation: After initially studying painting in Cairo, my focus shifted toward the structural challenge of three-dimensional design. This path led me to Germany, where I completed my Master's degree in jewellery design and spent several years working within the technical precision of the craft.

Artist statement: Today, my practice bridges both worlds. My jewellery is built on geometric structure and a deliberate, slow design process, while the canvas gives me room to step away from that rigid logic through fast, raw movements and layers of acrylic.

Pull quote: One gives me the structure I need; the other allows me to break out of it.
